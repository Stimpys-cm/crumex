Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I visit the building before deciding?

Yes. Site visits are free of charge. Tell us your preferred dates and we confirm with the owner.

Are prices in U.S. dollars?

Each listing shows the currency the owner quotes (USD or MXN). Lease prices are per month; we also show the price per square foot.

What lease terms are usual?

Terms, increases, deposits and maintenance depend on each owner and are agreed in writing. We share the owner’s conditions before any commitment.

Can you find something that is not listed?

Yes. Send us your requirements (size, clear height, docks, power and target date) and we will look for options.
